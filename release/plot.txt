While exploring mountains, Narfi finds giants, after defeating him with his wit, Narfi finds out giant was guarding Relic, the legendary sword of FIre Giant Surtr Laevateinn, which is rumoured to be able to summon immense amount of lava and slay many immortal beasts.
But it seems Narfi cant wield the legendary sword as it is not responding to boy's commands. Narfi seeks out wise watchman of castle, Fiolsvith to understand the reason. Narfi's friend, Loftur wants to tag along his friend because he is interested in this story.
Meanwhile Vidofnir, giant Rooster is troubled with news of Laevateinn being found, as it is foretold that this sword will be the weapon which will slay immortal rooster.
Giant bird Vidofnir tells Surtr about his sword in order to kill the boy and steal the Relic afterwards.
